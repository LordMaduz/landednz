## 1. THE PROBLEM — PRECISELY DEFINED

Every year, over 130,000 people arrive in New Zealand as migrants. When they land,
they face an immediate avalanche of tasks with zero guidance on sequencing, 
priority, or context:

- Get an IRD number (NZ tax number) — without it, taxed at 45% emergency rate
- Open a bank account — requires NZ address, but no NZ address yet (circular problem)
- Find a rental — Auckland market moves so fast properties rent within 48 hours
- Register with a GP — waitlists exist, need to know which practices accept new patients
- Enrol kids in school — school zones are strict and non-obvious
- Get a NZ driver's licence — foreign licences only valid 12 months
- Understand Warrant of Fitness (WoF) for vehicles — uniquely NZ concept
- Connect to the right suburb — proximity to work, schools, public transport
- Build a social network — identified as one of hardest parts of migration
- Understand workplace culture — NZ has distinct norms around hierarchy

Each task touches a different government agency, different website, different form.
No single place ties them together intelligently.

**The core problem is not lack of information.**
The information exists on government websites.
**The core problem is lack of sequencing.**
Nobody tells migrants what to do first, what depends on what, 
or how long each step takes.

## 3. WHAT CAUSES THE PROBLEM — ROOT CAUSES

### Root Cause 1: Fragmentation
Government services are siloed across separate agencies:
- IRD (tax)
- MBIE (immigration policy)
- Immigration New Zealand (visa processing)
- ACC (accident compensation)
- MSD (social development, Work and Income)
- Ministry of Health (healthcare)
- Ministry of Education (schools)
- Local councils (rates, services, library cards)

No unified entry point. A new arrival must know which agency handles what
and navigate each separately — often discovering the right agency only after
contacting the wrong one first.

### Root Cause 2: Generic, One-Size-Fits-All Information
Government settlement information doesn't differentiate between:
- Visa types (AEWV vs partner visa vs Green List residency)
- Family situations (single vs family with children)
- Occupations (regulated professions vs general skilled workers)
- Cities (Auckland vs Wellington vs Christchurch have different realities)

A nurse needing Nursing Council registration before she can work has completely
different Week 1 priorities than a software engineer who can start immediately.
No existing resource accounts for this.

### Root Cause 3: No Sequencing Guidance
This is the most damaging root cause.

Examples of sequencing failures and their consequences:
- IRD not applied for on day 1 → first paycheck taxed at 45% emergency rate
- Bank account attempted before having NZ address → rejected, weeks of delay
- Lease signed before understanding school zones → wrong suburb, 
  can't enrol in desired school
- NZQA qualification assessment not started pre-arrival → 10–16 week delay 
  to employment for regulated professions
- Bond paid directly to landlord (not lodged with Tenancy Services) → 
  no legal recourse if landlord keeps it

### Root Cause 4: No Personalised Digital Product
Current "solutions" are:
- Static government webpages (comprehensive but un-navigable)
- PDFs and welcome packs from employers (generic, often outdated)
- NGO orientation programmes (limited capacity, geographically constrained)
- Facebook groups and Reddit threads (informal, unreliable, unmoderated)
- Word of mouth from existing migrant community (hit or miss)

None of these are AI-powered, personalised, or proactively sequenced.

## 4. WHY IS IT STILL UNSOLVED — EXISTING SOLUTIONS & GAPS

### Government: Immigration New Zealand
**What exists:**
- Live and Work NZ website (live-work.immigration.govt.nz)
- Settlement services directory
- Migrant Settlement and Integration Strategy (refreshed 2023)
- 5 settlement outcome areas: participation, health, housing, education, employment

**The gap:**
- Static webpages, no AI, no personalisation
- Strategy defines outcomes but doesn't guide individuals to achieve them
- 16 success indicators measured annually — at population level, not individual level
- Government incentive is to provide information, not optimise user experience
- No digital product built to execute the strategy at individual level

### NGOs: Belong Aotearoa (formerly Auckland Regional Migrant Services)
**What exists:**
- 20+ years supporting migrants and refugees in Auckland
- Playgroups, orientation programs, advocacy
- Community-based settlement support

**The gap:**
- Human-powered — cannot scale to 130,000 arrivals/year
- Geographically limited to Auckland suburbs they operate in
- Focused on vulnerable migrants — less relevant to skilled professional migrants
- No digital product, no AI

### Employer Settlement Information (Legal Requirement)
**What exists:**
- Accredited employers legally required to provide settlement information 
  to AEWV holders within 30 days of employment

**The gap:**
- Most employers provide a generic PDF or nothing at all
- No personalisation, no follow-up, no AI assistance
- Employer has no incentive to invest in quality settlement support
- Creates a B2B opportunity for LandedNZ (white-label product for employers)

### Informal Community Channels
**What exists:**
- Facebook groups (e.g. "Indians in Auckland", "Filipinos in NZ")
- Reddit (r/newzealand, r/auckland)
- WhatsApp community groups by nationality

**The gap:**
- Completely unstructured — no sequencing, no priority
- Advice quality varies wildly — often outdated or incorrect
- Cannot personalise to individual situation
- Reactive (answer questions) not proactive (anticipate needs)

### No AI-Powered Competitor Exists
After thorough research, no product was found that provides:
- AI-personalised settlement journey for NZ migrants
- Conversational interface for NZ settlement questions
- Proactive reminders based on individual timeline
- Integration of visa type, family situation, city, and occupation

**The competitive space is empty. This is the opportunity.**

## 5. HOW AI CHANGES THE EQUATION

### What Was Impossible Before LLMs
- Personalising settlement guidance to each individual at scale
- Understanding natural language questions about NZ-specific topics
- Generating dynamic checklists based on multiple input variables
- Providing conversational, contextual answers without a human advisor

### What AI Makes Possible Now

**1. Personalisation At Scale**
5 onboarding questions → completely unique settlement roadmap:
- Visa type (AEWV / partner / Green List / student)
- City (Auckland / Wellington / Christchurch)
- Family situation (single / couple / family with children)
- Occupation (regulated profession / general skilled / trade)
- Arrival date (determines urgency sequencing)

No two users get the same checklist.
Previously required a human settlement advisor. Now achievable instantly.

**2. Conversational Navigation**
Instead of searching 5 government websites, user types:
"How do I enrol my daughter in school in Remuera?"

LandedNZ responds with:
- School zone explanation specific to Remuera
- Which schools are in zone
- Enrollment steps and required documents
- Waitlist reality and timing
- Whether this affects suburb choice

This is the difference between a search engine and a trusted advisor.

**3. Proactive Nudging**
AI tracks settlement progress and sends timely reminders before problems occur:
- "You arrived 11 months ago. Your foreign driver's licence expires in 1 month.
  Here's how to convert it."
- "Your first NZ tax year ends March 31. Here's what you need to do for your IR3."
- "KiwiSaver: you've been defaulted into a conservative fund. 
  At your age, here's what that means for your retirement."

No static resource can do this. AI makes it automatic and personalised.

**4. Emotional Intelligence Layer**
Research shows migrants go through predictable emotional stages:
Fun → Fright → Flight → Fight → Fit

AI can acknowledge this journey contextually:
- Week 1: Energetic, task-focused tone
- Week 3: Recognise the "fright" phase, provide reassurance
- Month 2: Celebrate milestones, reinforce belonging

No government website or PDF can do this.

## 6. THE PRODUCT OPPORTUNITY

### Product Name (Working Title)
**LandedNZ**

### Core Value Proposition
*"Your personalised guide to settling in New Zealand —
what to do, in what order, and why it matters."*

### MVP Feature Set (Phases 1–3 of Settlement Journey)

**Onboarding (5 questions, 2 minutes):**
- Visa type
- City
- Family situation
- Occupation / profession
- Arrival date (or expected arrival date)

**Core Output: Personalised Settlement Roadmap**
- Prioritised checklist specific to user's profile
- Each task includes: what it is, why it matters, how long it takes,
  what happens if delayed, and link to correct government resource
- Tasks grouped by urgency (this week / this month / in 3 months)

**AI Chat Interface**
- Ask any NZ settlement question in plain English
- Contextual answers based on user's profile
- Escalates to verified resource links when appropriate

**Progress Tracker**
- Mark tasks complete
- See what's unlocked next
- Visual progress through settlement phases

**Smart Reminders**
- Proactive notifications based on arrival date and task completion
- Timing-sensitive alerts (IRD urgency, licence expiry, tax year end)

### Monetisation Path

**B2C Freemium:**
- Free: Basic personalised checklist (Phases 1–2)
- Premium ($5–10 NZD/month): AI chat, smart reminders, Phase 3 content,
  community access

**B2B Employer (Phase 2 product expansion):**
- White-label version for NZ accredited employers
- Fulfils their legal obligation to provide settlement information
- Employers pay per migrant employee onboarded
- Potential pricing: $50–100 NZD per employee
- 17,500+ accredited employers in NZ market

### Platform Strategy
- Web app first (faster to build, cross-platform, no app store approval)
- iOS app second (after web validation, higher engagement for daily use)

## 8. RISKS & MITIGATIONS

### Risk 1: Government changes settlement resources significantly
**Likelihood:** Low — government pages exist but improvement is slow
**Mitigation:** LandedNZ's value is sequencing and personalisation, 
not the raw information. Even if government improves resources,
the AI personalisation layer remains the differentiator.

### Risk 2: AI gives incorrect settlement advice
**Likelihood:** Medium — LLMs can hallucinate
**Mitigation:** 
- All AI responses link to verified government source
- Clear disclaimer: "Always verify with official sources"
- AI designed to say "I'm not sure — check this link" rather than guess
- Community feedback loop to flag incorrect answers

### Risk 3: Low willingness to pay among migrants
**Likelihood:** Medium — migrants are often cost-conscious on arrival
**Mitigation:**
- Free tier provides genuine value (basic checklist)
- Premium tier priced at 1–2 coffees per month ($5–10 NZD)
- B2B employer channel removes dependence on individual payment
- Single most expensive mistake prevented (e.g. IRD delay = weeks of 45% tax)
  pays for years of subscription

### Risk 4: Hard to reach users before they arrive
**Likelihood:** Medium — users are in other countries pre-arrival
**Mitigation:**
- Pre-arrival phase is a core product feature (Phase 1)
- Target NZ immigration Facebook groups and forums where migrants research before arriving
- Partner with NZ immigration advisors and law firms as referral channel
- SEO: "moving to Auckland checklist", "NZ settlement guide" etc.
