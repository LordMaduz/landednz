Arrival · 20–30 minutes online

Get your IRD number

Without your IRD number and a tax code declaration, your employer deducts tax at 45% plus the earners' levy.4

Steps

  1. 1Register for myIR, Inland Revenue's online service. You don't need an IRD number to register.2
  2. 2In myIR, apply as a new arrival. Inland Revenue checks your identity with Immigration New Zealand.1
  3. 3Apply by your visa's final arrival date, the date you must arrive in New Zealand by. After that date you apply as living in New Zealand instead.1
  4. 4Inland Revenue's work and study page says new arrivals on work or student visas also need a New Zealand bank account or a customer due diligence form (IR997). Have one of them ready.3
  5. 5If your application is approved, your IRD number comes by text or email in 2 days.1
  6. 6Give your employer your IRD number with a completed tax code declaration (IR330).4
  7. 7Give your bank your IRD number too. Without it, tax on interest is deducted at 45%.5

Have ready

  • Your passport details1
  • Your Immigration New Zealand application number (not needed if you have an Australian passport)1
  • Your most recent overseas tax number, if you have one1

Good to know

  • Applying is free. Inland Revenue doesn't charge for an IRD number.6
  • Applying again when you've had an IRD number before. You keep the same number for life, so find your old one instead.7
  • If the new-arrival application doesn't work, Inland Revenue explains the other ways to apply.8
  • Inland Revenue's number for personal customers is 0800 227 774, or +64 4 832 5223 from overseas.9
