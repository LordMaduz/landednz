Guide rail — three variants

The left panel of the Guide full view. All three use your four picks; they differ only in how the rail looks. The reading column, header and composer are unchanged and shown only for context.

Your picks, and where each one shows

PickOn every variant
Wrap + wider at 1280 and upTitles wrap to 2 lines (tooltip after that); rail 320 px in the 1440 frames, 280 px in the 1024 frame. Nothing is cut mid-word.
Quiet Back link“Back to Home” / “Back to your task” as plain muted text with an arrow; no border, no fill. Collapse and × icons unchanged.
Title + “2 questions”Muted 12 px line under each title. Selected row: 3 px teal start bar (5.0:1 on white, above the 3:1 WCAG 1.4.11 needs). Weight does not change on select, so wrapping never shifts.

Sources for the values

  • Rail 320 px at ≥ 1280: Primer PageLayout pane “large” = 320 px (small 256, medium 296) — primer.style/product/components/page-layout. Below 1280 it stays 280 (current token).
  • Section labels 12 px / 600 / muted: Primer NavList group heading — primer.style/product/components/nav-list. Rows 14 (type role “ui”), counts 12 (role “small”). No new sizes.
  • Selected start bar: Primer NavList current item marker; contrast need from WCAG 2.2 SC 1.4.11 — w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/non-text-contrast.
  • JudgementBar width 3 px and 2-line clamp are my call; both are common practice, no system mandates the exact number.
