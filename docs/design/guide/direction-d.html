Guide · assistant pane

The guide lives beside your work. On Home, Roadmap and Task a small ask bar floats at the bottom of the page. Click it or press / and it grows into a quiet pane on the right, already holding what you're looking at. Answers read like a short checked note, with every source one tap away. One click widens it into a focused reading view.

Frames at 85%; the pane and state cards below are at 100%.

What makes it premium
Question set as the heading, answer as 16 px reading text. Inline numbered citations with previews. A crafted composer with the context chip, the day's count and a Send that becomes Stop. The pane floats inset with real depth instead of a hairline split.

Where you can ask

Only on Home, Roadmap and Task, where there is a roadmap to ask about. No Ask in the header and none in Settings. A small bar floats at the bottom of the page and already says what you'd be asking about. Click it or press / and it grows into the pane; close the pane and it settles back into the bar. Pages keep extra space at the bottom so the bar never sits on top of the last item.

The pane on a task

This task's earlier questions first, then the answer. The page moves left; nothing is covered.

Opening

Opened from the ask bar, / or Ask about this, the pane greets with where you are and three starters, with the cursor already in the composer. / works only when you are not typing in a field.

Writing, and the focused view

Expand turns the pane into a full-width reading view with the conversation grouped by task; collapse returns to the side.

  1. The pane always pushes; it never covers the page. From 1024 up the page moves aside. There is no band where it floats over Mark as done.
  2. Pages lay out by their own width. The page area is a size container, so Task and Home switch to their approved narrow layouts instead of being squeezed. With the pane closed nothing changes.
  3. One new layout, approved 30 Oct: while the pane is open, Task's actions column flexes between 300 and 380 (34% of the page area). It only ever gets wider as the window grows.
  4. Sizes in rem, a reading floor. Pane widths are in rem so a larger default text size scales with them. Task steps stay at about 45 characters a line or more.
  5. Nothing cut off, tested where it breaks. Playwright with the pane open on Task, Home and Roadmap at 1024, 1180, 1280, 1366, 1440, 1536, 1600, 1680 and 1920, plus 1366×768, a 20 px default font and 125% / 200% zoom: no sideways scroll, no clipped text, Mark as done never under the pane or the ask bar.
