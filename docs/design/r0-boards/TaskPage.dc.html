Apply for your IRD number

Done26 Oct

Without your IRD number and a tax code declaration, your employer deducts tax at 45% plus the earners' levy.1

Open the IRD application (opens in a new tab)

What you'll need

  • Your passport details
  • Your Immigration New Zealand application number
  • Your most recent overseas tax number, if you have one

Steps

  1. Register for myIR, Inland Revenue's online service. You don't need an IRD number to register.2
  2. In myIR, apply as a new arrival. Inland Revenue checks your identity with Immigration New Zealand.3
  3. Apply by your visa's final arrival date, the date you must arrive in New Zealand by. After that date you apply as living in New Zealand instead.3
  4. On a work or student visa you also need a New Zealand bank account or a customer due diligence form (IR997). Have one of them ready.4
  5. If your application is approved, your IRD number comes by text or email in 2 days.3
  6. Give your employer your IRD number with a completed tax code declaration (IR330).1
  7. Give your bank your IRD number too. Without it, tax on interest is deducted at 45%.5

Good to know

  • Had an IRD number before? You keep it for life, so find your old one instead of applying again.
  • Applying is free.
  • Inland Revenue's personal line is 0800 227 774, or +64 4 832 5223 from overseas.
