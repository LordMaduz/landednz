Arrival, 20 minutes

Get your IRD number

Your employer needs your IRD number and a tax code, or tax is taken at the highest rate.1

  1. Register for myIR, Inland Revenue’s online service.2
  2. Apply as a new arrival before your visa’s final arrival date, with your passport and Immigration NZ application number.2
  3. On a work or student visa, also have an NZ bank account or an IR997 form ready.3
  4. Give the number and a completed IR330 tax code form to your employer.1
