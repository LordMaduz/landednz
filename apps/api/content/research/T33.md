## Findings
INZ's arrival page lists help in your language (free Language Assistance Services), community links, the ethnic communities directory and CABs. INZ's Learning English page says ESOL courses come from many providers; pre-purchased classes are accessible at no extra cost through TEC-approved courses, enrolled with an Agreement and schedule. INZ's families page says costs depend on test results, with the maximum if no test is taken. Item counts: why 2, steps 7, documents 2, mistakes 2, watchOuts 3.
