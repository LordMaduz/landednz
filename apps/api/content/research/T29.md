# T29 Know your employment rights

## Questions
1. What written agreement and minimum rights does Employment NZ list for every employee? 2. Current minimum wage (stage 1: unconfirmed). 3. Payslips and records. 4. Holidays, leave, health and safety. 5. How to report migrant exploitation, phone number. 6. What Employment NZ and Immigration NZ publish about the Migrant Exploitation Protection Work Visa (MEPV).

## Sources used
All opened 2026-10-27 with curl (HTTP 200), raw text in research_notes/Phase 3 week 1 tasks/T29/.
- https://www.employment.govt.nz/starting-employment/employment-agreements — "Every employee must have a written employment agreement."
- https://www.employment.govt.nz/starting-employment/rights-and-responsibilities/employee-rights-and-responsibilities — "you do not have a written employment agreement (by law, your employer is required to give you one)"; leave table (4 weeks, 10 days sick leave); "provide a safe and healthy workplace ... you have the right to stop working if you believe it is unhealthy or unsafe"; "not demand to keep your passport".
- https://www.employment.govt.nz/pay-and-hours/pay-and-wages/minimum-wage/minimum-wage-rates-and-types — "effective from 1 April 2026"; Adult $23.95, Starting-out $19.16, Training $19.16; "0800 20 90 20".
- https://www.employment.govt.nz/pay-and-hours/pay-and-wages/payslips — "An employer does not have to give their employee a payslip unless their employment agreement says that they will get one."
- https://www.employment.govt.nz/resolving-problems/types-of-problems/migrant-exploitation — "report it to us online using our online form, or over the phone on 0800 200 088"; "Interpreters are available for over 180 different languages"; MEPV "valid for up to 6 months and cannot be extended"; "You must apply for the MEPV within 1 month of the date the letter was sent to you."

## Sources rejected
- https://www.immigration.govt.nz/visas/migrant-exploitation-protection-work-visa/ — Incapsula block (curl); WebFetch twice returned empty content. Unconfirmed, not cited.
- Guessed employment.govt.nz paths (hours-and-wages/..., holidays-and-leave, health-and-safety, pay-and-wages/minimum-wage): 404, not used.

## Uncertainties
- MEPV on Immigration NZ's own page: open it in a browser or retry later to confirm validity, fee and criteria.
- Stage 1's 0800 200 088 is confirmed live. Minimum wage confirmed ($23.95 adult from 1 April 2026).
- Health and safety: Employment NZ only gives a one-line duty; WorkSafe not an allowed source.
- Annual-holiday and sick-leave dedicated pages opened but not cited; settle by citing them if a reviewer wants more detail.

## Deliberately not said
- Whether the reader is eligible for the MEPV (visa advice).
- That payslips are required: the page says they are not required by law unless the agreement provides for them; the draft says only that records can be requested.
- Contractor/volunteer rights, trial periods, dismissal rules (out of scope).

## Findings
Employment NZ confirms written agreements, minimum wage, records, leave, safe workplace and exploitation reporting from live pages. Draft has 2 why, 8 steps (1 conditional), 2 documents, 2 mistakes, 4 watchOuts, 4 new sources, 6 new facts. All items verified: false.
