## Findings
- IRD page: one tax code per main source of income; the IR330 flowcharts pick it; give it to employer; new IR330 when code changes; IRD may ask employer to change an unsuitable code (e.g. M on two sources).
- 45% rate stated as "non-declaration rate" on the tax codes page and "non-notified rate ... (plus earners' levy)" on the IR330; both cited.
- Secondary codes SB/S/SH/ST/SA depend on total estimated annual income; not enumerated in the draft because thresholds change.
- IRD page and IRD levy page agree on $1.75 per $100 for 2026/27, up to $156,641.
- Items: why 2, steps 7 (one conditional SEEKING_WORK), documents 1, mistakes 2, watchOuts 3.

- Visa lines: none.

- Tax lines are informational and attributed to Inland Revenue or the IR330.
