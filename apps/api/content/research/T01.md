- What can you expect on first arrival (shared housing)?

## Findings

**Choosing a place.** INZ: "Where in New Zealand you choose to live might depend on your job or where you want to study. But other considerations may affect your decision." (https://www.immigration.govt.nz/live/setting-up-your-life-in-new-zealand/finding-somewhere-to-live/choosing-where-to-live-in-new-zealand/). The planning page says "Think about where you want to live, or where you might need to live for work." and "Use the NZ Ready tool to help plan your move to New Zealand." (https://www.immigration.govt.nz/live/moving-to-new-zealand/planning-your-move-to-new-zealand/).

**Cost varies by place.** "The cost of accommodation will vary from region to region." (INZ choosing page). "In larger cities like Auckland and Wellington, houses are much more expensive than in smaller places like Invercargill." (https://www.immigration.govt.nz/live/moving-to-new-zealand/cost-of-living-in-new-zealand/).

**Climate, heating, services.** "When you are choosing a house, check that it is insulated, how it is heated, and the amount of sun that it gets." (INZ choosing page). "New Zealand’s larger cities have well-developed public transport systems, but services can be limited in smaller centres" (same page). "The larger centres will have better access to the full range of medical services than the smaller towns and rural communities." (same page).

**Rent comparison.** INZ: "Use the Tenancy Services website to see what the rent is for different areas in New Zealand." (cost-of-living page). Tenancy Services: "The market rent amount listed is weekly rent." and "It is a useful guide for landlords and tenants but should not be used alone to determine the market rent of any property." (https://www.tenancy.govt.nz/rent-bond-and-bills/market-rent/). Data is "updated monthly and contains bond information from the previous 6 months (not including the most recent month)." (same page). "The data shown is for non-government owned properties that we have information on." (https://www.tenancy.govt.nz/rent-bond-and-bills/market-rent/market-rent-explained/).

**Rent frequency (pages differ in wording).** INZ: "In New Zealand, rent is typically charged each week" (cost-of-living page). Tenancy Services: "You will normally pay either weekly or fortnightly." (https://www.tenancy.govt.nz/starting-a-tenancy/new-to-tenancy/information-for-new-tenants/). Rent must be shown: "Rental properties cannot be advertised without a rental price listed" (https://www.tenancy.govt.nz/rent-bond-and-bills/rent/charging-rent/).

**Move-in cost.** INZ: "you may be charged up to 4 weeks of rent as bond (rental deposit)." (cost-of-living page). "It is unlawful for a landlord to ask the tenant to pay more than 2 weeks’ rent in advance." (charging-rent page). Tenancy Services example: "Your weekly rent is $500, the landlord requires 4 weeks’ rent as bond and 2 week’s rent in advance... The total move in cost is $3,000 ($2,000 + $1,000)." and "It’s important to work out the total move in cost before renting a property." (new-tenants page). Same page: "Work and Income New Zealand can help with bond and accommodation support in advance. You must meet the criteria to be eligible."

**First arrival.** "When you first arrive, you may share a house or apartment (sometimes called a flat) with other people." and "You may be limited to a single room in a house with other people." (cost-of-living page).

**School zones.** INZ: "If you want your child to attend a particular school, check if it has an 'enrolment zone'." (INZ choosing page); "Children living inside the zone for a school are guaranteed a place there." (https://www.immigration.govt.nz/live/setting-up-your-life-in-new-zealand/childcare-schools-and-higher-education/). Ministry of Education: "Some schools and kura have enrolment schemes. This means only students who live in a specific area around the school are guaranteed a place." and "Not all schools have zones. Anyone can enrol at schools that don't have an enrolment scheme." (https://www.education.govt.nz/parents-and-caregivers/schools-year-0-13/starting-school/school-zones-and-enrolment-schemes). Out of zone: "You may still be able to apply to enrol your child at a school if you live outside its enrolment zone. Your child is not guaranteed a place at the school." (same page). "You cannot temporarily move into a zone in order to get your child into a school." (same page). Checking an address: "check out the options near you on Education Counts. Their website includes the school zone information for schools that have one." (same page). FAQ: "The school may require proof of residence e.g. a tenancy agreement, certificate of title, or utility bills with an address." and "You will be able to see a copy of the scheme at the school." (https://web-assets.education.govt.nz/s3fs-public/2024-08/Enrolment%20Scheme%20FAQs_0.pdf). INZ on visa evidence: "You may need to provide the school with evidence of your child’s visa status." (INZ childcare-schools page).

**Public transport by city.** Auckland: Auckland Transport is "Responsible for public transport services including: bus, train and ferry services routes and timetables; fares, ticketing, HOP cards and contactless payments; information, journey planning and customer support." (https://at.govt.nz/about-us/changing-transport-delivery-in-auckland-transport-reform). Wellington: "Plan your journey around Wellington city on buses, trains and ferries with Metlink." (https://www.gw.govt.nz/transport/metlink-bus-train-and-ferry/). General: "Most towns, cities and regions have websites with information and timetables to help you plan your trip." (https://www.immigration.govt.nz/live/setting-up-your-life-in-new-zealand/getting-around-in-new-zealand/).

[conditional: family = FAMILY_WITH_CHILDREN or SOLE_PARENT] — https://www.education.govt.nz/parents-and-caregivers/schools-year-0-13/starting-school/school-zones-and-enrolment-schemes

**documents**

**mistakes**
1. Using the market rent figure alone: Tenancy Services says it "should not be used alone to determine the market rent of any property". — https://www.tenancy.govt.nz/rent-bond-and-bills/market-rent/
2. Moving into a zone only temporarily to get a school place: the Ministry of Education says "You cannot temporarily move into a zone in order to get your child into a school." [conditional: family = FAMILY_WITH_CHILDREN or SOLE_PARENT] — https://www.education.govt.nz/parents-and-caregivers/schools-year-0-13/starting-school/school-zones-and-enrolment-schemes
3. Not working out the total move-in cost (bond plus rent in advance) before renting. — https://www.tenancy.govt.nz/starting-a-tenancy/new-to-tenancy/information-for-new-tenants/

**watchOuts**
1. Houses do not normally have central heating, and INZ points to the healthy homes standards for rentals on heating, insulation and ventilation. — https://www.immigration.govt.nz/live/setting-up-your-life-in-new-zealand/finding-somewhere-to-live/choosing-where-to-live-in-new-zealand/
2. Public transport services can be limited in smaller centres, and in rural areas you may have to rely on a car. — https://www.immigration.govt.nz/live/setting-up-your-life-in-new-zealand/finding-somewhere-to-live/choosing-where-to-live-in-new-zealand/
3. When you first arrive you may share a house or flat, possibly with a single room only; INZ says to keep this in mind if bringing a partner or family. — https://www.immigration.govt.nz/live/moving-to-new-zealand/cost-of-living-in-new-zealand/
4. The market rent tool covers non-government-owned properties with lodged bonds in the last 6 months, so it is a guide only. — https://www.tenancy.govt.nz/rent-bond-and-bills/market-rent/market-rent-explained/
5. Not all schools have zones, and an out-of-zone child is not guaranteed a place; INZ and the Ministry of Education say the same. [conditional: family = FAMILY_WITH_CHILDREN or SOLE_PARENT] — https://www.education.govt.nz/parents-and-caregivers/schools-year-0-13/starting-school/school-zones-and-enrolment-schemes

**cost**
1. Rent is shown per week in the Tenancy Services tool; INZ says it is "typically charged each week" while Tenancy Services says you will "normally pay either weekly or fortnightly". — https://www.tenancy.govt.nz/rent-bond-and-bills/market-rent/ ; https://www.immigration.govt.nz/live/moving-to-new-zealand/cost-of-living-in-new-zealand/ ; https://www.tenancy.govt.nz/starting-a-tenancy/new-to-tenancy/information-for-new-tenants/

3. A landlord cannot lawfully ask for more than 2 weeks' rent in advance. — https://www.tenancy.govt.nz/rent-bond-and-bills/rent/charging-rent/ — Fact: 2 weeks; "It is unlawful for a landlord to ask the tenant to pay more than 2 weeks’ rent in advance."; that URL; type A.
4. Tenancy Services' worked example: $500 weekly rent with 4 weeks' bond and 2 weeks in advance gives a $3,000 move-in cost. — https://www.tenancy.govt.nz/starting-a-tenancy/new-to-tenancy/information-for-new-tenants/ — Fact: $3,000; "The total move in cost is $3,000 ($2,000 + $1,000)."; that URL; type A (agency example, not a market figure).
5. Market rent data covers bonds from the previous 6 months. — https://www.tenancy.govt.nz/rent-bond-and-bills/market-rent/ — Fact: 6 months; "contains bond information from the previous 6 months (not including the most recent month)."; that URL; type B.
