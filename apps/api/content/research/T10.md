## Uncertainties

- Whether an IRD number is required to join: no opened page says so. Settle by reading the KS2 form PDF.
- Employer rate wording differs between IRD pages (3.5% vs stale 3%); settle when IRD updates the "Growing" page.
- Transfer time: IRD "about 2 weeks"; Sorted "10 to 35 days". Both official; only IRD cited.
- legislation.govt.nz (KiwiSaver Act 2006) not tried; no claim depends on it.

## Deliberately not said

- That any visa holder is or is not eligible, beyond Inland Revenue's published wording.
- Provider names, fees, "best" funds or a recommended fund type: Inland Revenue says it cannot give financial advice.
- Transfer time of 10 to 35 days (Sorted) alongside IRD's figure.
- Any NZ Super comparison or ESCT rates (not read on an opened page).

## Findings

- Eligibility: citizens or people entitled to live in NZ indefinitely who live or normally live here; temporary, visitor, work and student visa holders cannot join.
- Rates (live now): 3.5%, 4%, 6%, 8%, 10%; default 3.5% from 1 April 2026; default rises to 4% on 1 April 2028; employer minimum 3.5%.
- Government contribution: 25 cents per dollar, max $260.72, full amount at $1042.86 contributed (1 July to 30 June); income limit $180,000; from 1 July 2025.
- Opt-out only for automatic enrolment, day 14 to day 56; fund changes any time; provider switch about 2 weeks.
- Draft counts: why 3, steps 9, documents 2, mistakes 2, watchOuts 5, advice 1.
