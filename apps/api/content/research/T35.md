## Questions
1. Who can get Working for Families and how do you apply?
2. What is the trade-off with the temporary (transitional) tax exemption?
3. What residency conditions apply to the adult and the child?

## Uncertainties
- Income thresholds and payment rates: not read (rates pages not opened). Settled by reading /types/* and /estimate-your-entitlement; none are in T35.
- The two sets of residency requirements ("1 will do") interact with the transitional-resident condition; the page does not say which set a child-qualifying family uses. Settled by IRD's eligibility tool or the page author.

## Deliberately not said
- Whether to claim or keep the exemption (no advice).
- That the user is or is not eligible.

## Findings
IRD says WfF and the temporary tax exemption cannot be held together; applying ends the exemption irrecoverably; partners are caught too; you can apply once the exemption ends. Applying is via myIR. Resident visa needed (temporary visa alone does not qualify).

## Compliance

- No visa lines assess anyone's own case. T35.mistakes.visa-only quotes IRD's entitlement rule.
- No line tells the reader they are or are not eligible. Every eligibility line is attributed ("Inland Revenue says…"). The partner line ("the other cannot apply") repeats IRD's rule word for word, so it does not assess anyone.
- The tax lines are informational. IRD does not tell people to get professional advice. It says "we recommend you consider your options before you apply", and T35.watchOuts.consider covers that.
- There are no persona names and no first person.
- **Step order (risk of losing the exemption):** the steps array starts with T35.steps.register, ahead of the exemption checks (tte-status, partner, tte-warning). Someone who reads from the top, or a UI that shows only the first step, could register first, and registering ends the exemption permanently ("you cannot change your mind and reinstate your previous TTE"). The opening clause "When you've done the checks below" softens this but does not remove the risk. IRD's own register page lists "to know whether you or your partner has a temporary tax exemption" under "Before you start". Recommended order: tte-status → partner → tte-warning → residency → child → prepare → register → notice. Once register comes after the checks, change "below" to "above". The wording of each line is fine. Only the order is the problem.
