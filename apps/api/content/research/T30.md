## Uncertainties
- INZ "priority" line: settled by opening the live INZ page from an unblocked browser (human check).
- Exactly which dependent children of visa holders count as domestic: settled by reading the current Education (Domestic Student: Primary and Secondary Education) Notice 2026 (MoE's enrolling-overseas page still names the 2023 Notice).
- Govt.nz says free state schooling for citizens/permanent residents; MoE says state and state-integrated, domestic student includes citizen or resident-class visa holders. Left as each page's wording.

## Deliberately not said
- Whether any particular visa makes a child domestic or international.
- Fees for international students (set by each school's board).
- Ballot dates (Auckland 2026 only).
- Home education rules; children without valid visa (separate MoE process).

## Findings
- Compulsory 6 to 16; most start at 5; cohort entry at some schools.
- Zone guarantee, proof of residence, no temporary moves, out-of-zone applications not guaranteed.
- Documents: proof of age, medical, legal, contact, proof of address; MoE appendix for residents.
- Domestic/international: state MoE wording and point to MoE contact.

### Compliance

- **Visa and eligibility lines.** steps.visa-status, documents.passport-residence-visa and watchOuts.dependent-children repeat the Ministry's published information and send the reader to the school or the Ministry. None of them assesses a particular child's case. No line tells the reader they are or are not eligible. steps.check-zone quotes the Ministry's general rule ("automatically qualifies") on the condition that the family lives in the zone, and that is how the page states it.
- **Advice item.** An advice item is present: the Ministry contact for domestic-student eligibility. Both MoE eligibility pages tell people to contact the Ministry, and Govt.nz's "contact your nearest Ministry of Education office" appears in watchOuts.refusal. The items contain no tax, tenancy or legal advice.
- **Names and voice.** No persona names. No first person.
- **Page age (observation only).** The govt-nz page was last updated on 07 March 2024. Its content agrees with the more recent MoE pages, which were updated in August and October 2026.
