## Uncertainties

- INZ's own utilities line (power, internet) could not be read, so the utilities step cites Tenancy Services. Settles: INZ page readable via WebFetch or owner check.
- The bond-lodged "6 weeks" tenant check is a recommendation on the preventing-problems page; the contact-us page says Tenancy Services processes lodgements in up to 10 working days. Both are stated separately, no conflict.
- `processingTime: 4 weeks` kept from the legacy file; no cited page supports it. Settles: owner decides to drop it.
- Tribunal application fee ($28 on the contact page) and the Tribunal process pages were not used; fee may change.

## Deliberately not said

- Legacy claims dropped (no official page read): Auckland market advice, Tapi/Palace/Renti platforms, rental scam signs, "visa status does not affect tenancy rights", $550 cost, powerswitch/broadbandcompare links, 4-week bond-receipt rule (page says 6 weeks).
- Rent in advance max: left in T01.
- No eligibility statement on Work and Income bond help (page only says criteria apply).

## Findings

- Agreements must be in writing; landlord must give signed copy before the tenancy starts; Act applies anyway.
- Bond up to 4 weeks' rent (general), receipt required, lodged within 23 working days; Tenant Number kept private.
- Letting fees banned for tenants; key money banned except option fee up to one week's rent.
- Healthy homes compliance statement, insurance and insulation statements required in new agreements.
- Inspection report signed by both is evidence; tenant cannot be blamed for recorded damage.
- Dispute options: self-resolution, FastTrack, mediation, Tribunal; info line 0800 836 262.
