## Uncertainties

- **Do work-visa new arrivals need a NZ bank account or IR997?** The IRD work-or-study page (updated 03 Sep 2026) says student and work visa new arrivals "also need 1 of these requirements", meaning a bank account or IR997. The IRD new-arrival page (updated 11 Sep 2026) lists neither. What would settle it: the live myIR new-arrival form, or a written answer from IRD on 0800 227 774.
- **"2 days": working or calendar?** The new-arrival page does not say. What would settle it: an IRD statement, or timing observed in a real application.
- **Is over-deducted ND-rate tax refunded at year end?** IRD's assessment page covers refunds for people whose only income is wages or taxed investment income. No opened page addresses ND-rate cases or part-year new arrivals with overseas income. What would settle it: an IRD page or IRD confirmation that covers this case.
- **What does the myIR form ask?** The form is behind a login, so its fields and any inline bank-account check are unverified. What would settle it: a walkthrough with a test myIR account.
- **Circular bank requirement.** INZ says you need an IRD number to open a bank account, while IRD (offshore route, and possibly work visas) asks for a bank account to get an IRD number. IRD's 45% no-number interest rule shows that accounts can exist without one. What would settle it: an IRD or INZ statement on which comes first. Individual bank policies are out of scope.
- **Can you apply for the new-arrival route before landing?** INZ says yes. The new-arrival page describes people who "have recently arrived" and does not say. What would settle it: IRD confirmation, or trying it in myIR from offshore.
- **Overseas phone number.** The phone-numbers page says +64 4 832 5223. The find-my-number page says +64 4 831 2249. What would settle it: IRD confirmation. Use the phone-numbers page meanwhile.
- **Quote wording.** Some pages were read through a summarising fetch tool: new arrival, tax codes, work or study (in part). What would settle it: re-check the quotes against the live pages before publishing.

## Findings

**Who needs one and why.** IRD: "You need an IRD number for any tax-related obligations you have or entitlements you get". Its examples include earning income, "opening a bank account" and "joining KiwiSaver" (https://www.ird.govt.nz/managing-my-tax/ird-numbers/ird-numbers-for-individuals/when-you-need-one). Without one, "you'll pay a higher tax rate of 45%" (same URL). An incomplete IR330 means "the non-notified rate of 45% (plus earners' levy)" (https://www.ird.govt.nz/-/media/project/ir/home/documents/forms-and-guides/ir300---ir399/ir330/ir330.pdf?modified=20250324034423&modified=20250324034423). The employer guide puts the combined figure at "46.75% (includes ACC earners' levy)", tax code ND (https://www.ird.govt.nz/-/media/project/ir/home/documents/forms-and-guides/ir300---ir399/ir335/ir335.pdf?modified=20251021003937). The ACC earners' levy for 2026/27 is "$1.75 per $100 (1.75%)", up to maximum earnings of $156,641 (https://www.ird.govt.nz/income-tax/income-tax-for-individuals/acc-clients-and-carers/acc-earners-levy-rates). Bank interest without an IRD number is taxed "at a rate of 45%". With a number you choose a rate, and the default is 33% (https://www.ird.govt.nz/income-tax/withholding-taxes/resident-withholding-tax-rwt/using-the-right-rwt-tax-rate). INZ: without an IRD number "you will be taxed at the highest possible rate" (https://www.immigration.govt.nz/live/setting-up-your-life-in-new-zealand/managing-your-money-banking-and-taxes-in-new-zealand/paying-taxes-in-new-zealand/). Year-end: IRD sends an assessment that "shows if you are due a refund" to people whose only income is wages or taxed investment income. No opened page confirms this for ND-rate cases (https://www.ird.govt.nz/income-tax/income-tax-for-individuals/what-happens-at-the-end-of-the-tax-year/income-tax-assessments).

**New-arrival route.** The route is for "people who have recently arrived in New Zealand". IRD checks your identity with INZ, so you send no documents (https://www.ird.govt.nz/managing-my-tax/ird-numbers/ird-numbers-for-individuals/new-arrival-to-new-zealand---ird-number-application). It needs a myIR account. Registration needs a name, email, phone, user ID, a 12 to 255 character password and a second verification method, with no IRD number required (https://www.ird.govt.nz/myir-help/register-account/personal). What IRD asks for by visa type (new-arrival URL above):

- Australian passport: passport details.
- Resident visa: passport details and INZ application number.
- Work or student visa: passport details, INZ application number, and "your most recent overseas tax number (if you have one)".

You can also apply for a child's number if your residence visa includes the child, or if the child has a student visa and you hold a guardianship visa for them (same URL).

**Bank account / IR997.** The new-arrival page has no mention of a bank account or IR997 (new-arrival URL above). The work-or-study page says work and student visa new arrivals "also need 1 of these requirements", meaning a "fully functioning New Zealand bank account" or "IR997". Resident visa and Australian passport holders "will not need either" (https://www.ird.govt.nz/situations/work-or-study-in-new-zealand). This is unresolved; see Uncertainties. INZ says "To open a bank account in New Zealand, you need an IRD number" (INZ paying-taxes URL above).

**Before arrival and deadline.** INZ: "You can apply for an IRD number before you arrive in New Zealand" (https://www.immigration.govt.nz/live/setting-up-your-life-in-new-zealand/when-you-arrive-in-new-zealand/). IRD: applying as a new arrival "before your visa travel date expires can be a faster option". It advises applying "as soon as you can once you're in New Zealand" (work-or-study URL above). Your visa's final arrival date "is also the date you must apply for an IRD number by if you want to use this process". Australian passport holders "often have 4 to 6 weeks after you arrive". After the deadline you apply as "living in New Zealand" (new-arrival URL above).

**Delivery and fee.** "If approved, you'll receive your IRD number by text or email in 2 days." IRD contacts you if it needs more information (new-arrival URL above). Fee: "There is no cost when you apply for an IRD number with us" (when-you-need-one URL above). Also: "You do not need to pay us when you apply for an IRD number" (https://www.ird.govt.nz/managing-my-tax/ird-numbers/ird-numbers-for-individuals).

**Other routes.** If the new-arrival application "did not work", use "living in New Zealand" or "living overseas" (https://www.ird.govt.nz/managing-my-tax/ird-numbers/ird-numbers-for-individuals/cannot-get-my-ird-number-online).

- **Living in NZ.** Apply in myIR, then you have "20 days to take your identification documents to an AA Driver Licensing Agent". You need one primary document (for example an overseas passport plus NZ visa; an Australian passport needs no visa) and one secondary document (for example an overseas driver licence). Documents must be physical, and non-English documents need certified translation. The paper IR595 can be verified at an AA agent or an Inland Revenue office. Processing takes "within 10 working days if you apply online, or 12 working days" on paper. If you cannot supply the ID, call 0800 775 247 (https://www.ird.govt.nz/managing-my-tax/ird-numbers/ird-numbers-for-individuals/living-and-new-zealand-and-not-a-new-arrival---ird-number-application).
- **Living overseas.** Form IR742, emailed with scans. You need photo ID, proof of address, your home-country TIN, proof of your reason, and "a fully functioning New Zealand bank account or a Customer due diligence - IR997 form". IRD "might be able to accept different information" if you cannot get bank documents. It aims to process "within 20 days", and posting "can take 20 days to arrive". The page suggests future arrivals "apply for an IRD number as a new arrival when you get here" (https://www.ird.govt.nz/managing-my-tax/ird-numbers/ird-numbers-for-individuals/living-overseas---ird-number-application).

**Had one before.** "You keep the same IRD number for life so, even if you lose it, you do not need to apply for a new one." The online lookup needs your full name, date of birth, and a NZ mobile, email or bank account already registered with IRD. You can also find the number on payslips, IRD letters or KiwiSaver statements, or by calling (https://www.ird.govt.nz/managing-my-tax/ird-numbers/find-my-ird-number).

**Who to give it to.** Employer: "your IRD number; the right tax code". Without them you pay the "'no-notification' rate" (work-or-study URL above), declared on the IR330 (IR330 URL above). Bank or other interest payer: give it to avoid 45% RWT (RWT URL above). KiwiSaver: "joining KiwiSaver" needs an IRD number (when-you-need-one URL above).

**Contacts.** Personal lines: 0800 227 774 within NZ and +64 4 832 5223 from overseas, "Monday to Friday, 8am to 6pm" (https://www.ird.govt.nz/contact-us/general/phone-numbers). The find-my-number page lists +64 4 831 2249 for overseas callers (find-my-number URL above); see Uncertainties.
