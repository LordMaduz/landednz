## Findings

**INZ points to a page on checking conditions.** The INZ form page has a heading "Check if you can change your work visa conditions" and the line "Before applying, check if you can change your work visa conditions or your job, and what you need." Its link text is "Check or change your work visa conditions". The linked page itself was blocked and is not a source (https://www.immigration.govt.nz/formshelp/application-for-a-variation-of-conditions).

**Conditions INZ says can be changed.** "You can apply to: change occupation, employer, and location of employment remove conditions if you hold a partnership-based work visa subject to work conditions, or study part-time." The list items run together in the page text (https://www.immigration.govt.nz/formshelp/application-for-a-variation-of-conditions).

**Which form.** "If you hold an Accredited Employer Work Visa (AEWV), Peak Seasonal Visa or Global Workforce Seasonal Visa, use this form to apply for a Job Change. If you hold a different work visa, use this form to apply for a variation of conditions." Visitor and student visas use another form: "If you want to apply for a variation of travel conditions, have a visitor or student visa, you need to use form INZ 1020." (https://www.immigration.govt.nz/formshelp/application-for-a-variation-of-conditions). The INZ 1020 PDF was not opened.

**Conditions bind the holder.** "The holder of a visa that is subject to conditions must comply with the conditions of the visa." and "the obligation to comply with those conditions arises whether or not the visa holder is aware of the conditions, or of the implications of not complying with them." (Immigration Act 2009 s 56(1),(2), https://www.legislation.govt.nz/act/public/2009/51/en/latest/, as at 10 July 2026).

**Breach can lead to deportation liability.** "A temporary entry class visa holder is liable for deportation if the Minister determines that there is sufficient reason to deport the temporary entry class visa holder." and "sufficient reason includes, but is not limited to,— (a) breach of conditions of the person's visa" (Immigration Act 2009 s 157(1),(5), https://www.legislation.govt.nz/act/public/2009/51/en/latest/, as at 10 July 2026).

**Only licensed or exempt people may give immigration advice.** "No person may provide immigration advice unless that person— (a) is licensed under this Act to provide that advice; or (b) is exempt under section 11 from the requirement to be licensed." (Immigration Advisers Licensing Act 2007 s 6, https://www.legislation.govt.nz/act/public/2007/15/en/latest/, as at 27 November 2025).

**Pointing to INZ or to a list of advisers is not advice.** Immigration advice "does not include— (i) providing information that is publicly available, or that is prepared or made available by the Department; or (ii) directing a person to the Minister or the Department, or to an immigration officer ... or to a list of licensed immigration advisers" (s 7(1), https://www.legislation.govt.nz/act/public/2007/15/en/latest/). This supports the pointer design of T28.

**Some people are exempt from licensing.** Section 11 lists "lawyers and employees of lawyers and of incorporated law firms" and "persons employed by or working as volunteers for citizens advice bureaux", among others, including "persons who provide immigration advice in an informal or family context only, so long as the advice is not provided systematically or for a fee" (s 11, https://www.legislation.govt.nz/act/public/2007/15/en/latest/). The INZ form page lists the same categories and says licence numbers "must start with 20, followed by 7 numbers, for example 20123456." (https://www.immigration.govt.nz/formshelp/application-for-a-variation-of-conditions).

**The register is public.** "A person may search the register for a purpose set out in section 77(2)." (s 80(1), https://www.legislation.govt.nz/act/public/2007/15/en/latest/). The register's web address is not confirmed by an opened page.

## Compliance

- Visa lines repeat Immigration NZ's published information or the Acts, credited as "Immigration New Zealand says" or "The Immigration Act 2009 says". None of them assesses the reader's own case.
- No line tells the reader they are or are not eligible or qualify. The conditional lines ("If you hold …") match the visa groups the page names. The two exceptions are the documents and accredited-employer rows above, which drop a condition.
- An advice item is present: the licensed adviser line, citing the IAL Act 2007. The INZ page has no explicit "get advice" instruction, so `required: false` is consistent with the source.
- There are no persona names. The text uses second person ("you") throughout and never first person.
