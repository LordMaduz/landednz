## Uncertainties
- Visa/residency condition for 20 Hours ECE: none stated on the handbook 4-1 or parent pages read. Would be settled by a Ministry page stating it (handbook 4-2 child eligibility or the Ministry of Education contact), or Ministry confirmation. Draft is silent.
- INZ page says 20 Hours is for ages 3 and 4; MoE says 3, 4 or 5. Settled by INZ updating its page.

## Deliberately not said
- That a child of any visa status can or cannot get 20 Hours ECE (no page states it).
- "Fully funded for ages 3 and 4" (INZ wording, contradicted by the Ministry's own pages on age 5).
- Price of ECE or the Childcare Subsidy rates (not read; rates page not opened).
- The Work and Income citizenship/permanent resident condition as applying to the family: stated only as that page's wording.

## Findings
- ECE is not compulsory (INZ). Types: kindergarten, education and care, home-based, kōhanga reo, Playcentre, playgroups, Te Kura (MoE).
- Enrol directly with a service, any child from birth until 6; services set own process and may have waiting lists (MoE).
- 20 Hours ECE: age 3, 4 or 5; up to 20 hours a week, up to 6 a day, splittable between services; claimed via the service's enrolment form; eligibility begins on the third birthday and ends the day before the sixth birthday or last day before school (MoE).
- Childcare Subsidy cannot cover the same hours as 20 Hours ECE except home-based top ups (MoE handbook, Work and Income).
