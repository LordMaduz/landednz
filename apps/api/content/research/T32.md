## Uncertainties
- Child age limit for eligibility: eligibility guide says "17 years old or younger"; schedule pages say "under 18"; zero-fees page mixes 13 and 17. Settled by Health NZ correcting the zero-fees page.
- Well Child age: two ranges on one page (6 weeks to 5 years vs visits 4 weeks to 4 years).
- No enrolment steps for Well Child on its own page beyond the providers list; how referral works is unconfirmed.
- Whether Health NZ's dental service is formally named Community Oral Health Service: not on the page.
- Overseas records: only the catch-up page mentions them; no page says "bring" them.

## Deliberately not said
- GP zero-fees age (13 vs 17) and prescriptions: Health NZ's page contradicts itself.
- "Community Oral Health Service" name; school dental clinic details.
- Whether a specific visa qualifies a child: pointed to the eligibility guide.

## Findings
Well Child is free and runs from around 6 weeks to 5 years; provider list has Plunket and regional providers. Scheduled immunisations are free, catch-ups free under 18, bookable online/phone/provider. Dental care is publicly funded to the 18th birthday, enrol via Talk Teeth. Child eligibility for immunisation and Well Child is stated as regardless of citizenship or immigration status.
