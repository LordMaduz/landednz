## Findings
- Enrolment is free; enrolled fee is subsidised, casual rate higher (Health NZ).
- Only people eligible for publicly funded services can enrol (Govt.nz). Eligibility itself is linked to the Health NZ guide, not repeated (T22).
- Work visa test of 2 years or more is stated by Health NZ's guide, including time already legally here.
- Fully booked practice: refer to PHO (Health NZ and Govt.nz agree).
- ACC: Health NZ says it covers accident treatment regardless of residential status; injury visits usually cost less.
- LMC: choose as soon as pregnancy is confirmed (Govt.nz); non-citizens may get free or subsidised care (Govt.nz).

### Compliance

- The visa lines (steps.work-visa, documents.work-visa) state only Health NZ's published criteria, attributed to it, and point to "Read the guide for your own situation". They do not assess anyone's case. None of these lines cites Immigration NZ.
- No line tells a person they are or are not eligible. steps.work-visa repeats the guide's general rule ("you are eligible if your visa lets you stay…") and attributes it to the guide. This is acceptable, but "the guide says people on a work visa are eligible if…" would avoid the second-person eligibility wording entirely. This is optional.
- No tax, tenancy or legal advice. The sources do not tell readers to get professional advice, so no advice item is needed.
- No persona names and no first person.
