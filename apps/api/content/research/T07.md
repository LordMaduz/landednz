## Findings

**Identity and address.** The AML/CFT Act sets what a bank must obtain: "(a) the person's full name; and (b) the person's date of birth; ... (d) the person's address or registered office" (https://www.legislation.govt.nz/act/public/2009/0035/latest/whole.html, section 15, page shows "Version as at 1 July 2026"). Section 16(1)(a) requires the bank to "take reasonable steps to satisfy itself that the information obtained under section 15 (other than the information obtained under section 15(d)) is correct". Address is excluded from that check. The footnote reads "Section 16(1)(a): amended, on 27 November 2025, by section 10 of the Statutes Amendment Act 2025". Timing: "a reporting entity must carry out verification of identity before establishing a business relationship" (section 16(2), same URL).

**Address rule context.** The Ministry of Justice lists the related change as "Relaxing the address verification requirement under standard CDD — Page 19, new regulation 24AJ 'Exemption from address verification requirement'" (https://www.justice.govt.nz/justice-sector-policy/key-initiatives/aml-cft/development-of-the-amlcft-act-and-regulations/aml-cft-review/). That page describes the regulation route. The Act text above is the in-force wording used here.

**IRD number and interest.** "interest payments are taxed at the non-declaration rate if you have not given your IRD number to the interest payer – from 1 April 2020, the non-declaration rate is 45%." (https://www.ird.govt.nz/income-tax/withholding-taxes/resident-withholding-tax-rwt). Same fact: "If you have not given your IRD number to your interest payer, they will deduct tax from interest paid to you at a rate of 45%" (https://www.ird.govt.nz/income-tax/withholding-taxes/resident-withholding-tax-rwt/using-the-right-rwt-tax-rate). With a number: "you may use the 10.5%, 17.5%, 30%, 33% or 39% rate... If you do not choose a RWT rate, tax will be deducted from your interest payments at 33%." Also: "If the RWT rate you choose does not match your income tax rate you may receive an end of year tax bill." (same URL). Inland Revenue may correct a wrong number: "We might tell your investment Income payer (such as your bank) your correct IRD number if you have given them an incorrect IRD number." (same URL). The official fact is about the tax rate on interest. No official page found says the number is needed to open an account.

**Joint accounts and tax.** "Investment income from joint accounts is split equally across all account holders, if the account holder has given a valid IRD number to their payer." (https://www.ird.govt.nz/income-tax/withholding-taxes/resident-withholding-tax-rwt). "If you have a joint account, you can only use one RWT rate." (https://www.ird.govt.nz/income-tax/withholding-taxes/resident-withholding-tax-rwt/using-the-right-rwt-tax-rate). These are tax facts only.

**Deposit cover.** The Deposit Takers Act 2023 sets the compensation entitlement as "the lesser of ... (b) $100,000 or a temporary high balance limit (if any)." Its joint example reads "A and A's spouse jointly hold 2 other deposits ... A's share ... is $45,000 (an equal share of $90,000)." (https://www.legislation.govt.nz/act/public/2023/0035/latest/whole.html, "Latest version as at 31 July 2025").

**Scams and overseas money.** "Most scams come from overseas, but they may pretend to be locally based — from a reputable company, bank, or government agency... It is very difficult to get your money back once it is sent overseas." (https://www.consumerprotection.govt.nz/general-help/scamwatch).

**Credit reports.** "Three independent agencies hold credit data in New Zealand: Centrix, Equifax and Experian. You're entitled to a free copy of your credit report from each" (https://sorted.org.nz/guides/tackling-debt/credit-reports/). Also: "Everyone has a legal right to request information held about us by a credit reporting agency, at no cost." and "checking your own credit report doesn't affect your score at all. It's called a 'soft enquiry'... when a third party (like a bank, landlord or employer) checks your credit, it shows up as a 'hard enquiry'". Timing: "The free reports typically take up to 10 working days to arrive. If you need one urgently (within five working days), you may need to pay a fee." (same URL).

**Complaints.** "All businesses that offer financial services to retail customers must belong to an independent dispute resolution scheme." and "There are four approved dispute resolution schemes... Banking Ombudsman Scheme - considers complaints about all the main banks" and "the dispute resolution scheme can provide a free, independent view about a fair outcome to your complaint." (https://www.fma.govt.nz/consumer/everyday-finance/disputes-and-consumer-protection/).
