## What LandedNZ keeps

- Your name and email address, from Google when you sign in.
- Your five onboarding answers: visa, city, who is moving with you, your work and your arrival date.
- Which tasks you have marked done, and when.
- The questions you ask the assistant and its answers.

## How it is used

To build your roadmap, keep your progress, and answer your questions about it. LandedNZ does not sell your information or use it for advertising.

## Who else handles it

Your questions and the parts of your roadmap they need are sent to Anthropic to write the assistant's answers. Your account and roadmap are stored with `[hosting provider and region]`.

## Your rights

Under the Privacy Act 2020 you can ask to see the information LandedNZ holds about you and ask for it to be corrected. You can delete your account and everything in it from Settings at any time.
