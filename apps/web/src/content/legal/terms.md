## What LandedNZ is

LandedNZ builds a settling-in roadmap for your first months in New Zealand from your five onboarding answers, and answers questions about it. It is run by `[operator name]`.

## General information, not advice

The roadmap and the assistant's answers are general information, checked against official sources on the dates shown. They are not legal, immigration or tax advice. Before you act on anything important, check the official source linked from the task or ask a licensed adviser.

## Your account

You sign in with Google. Keep your Google account secure, because anyone who can sign in to it can see your roadmap. You can delete your account and everything in it from Settings at any time.

## Acceptable use

Use LandedNZ for your own move. Do not try to break it, overload it, or get the assistant to produce harmful content. LandedNZ may close an account that does.

## Changes

LandedNZ may change these terms. The date at the top shows when they last changed.
